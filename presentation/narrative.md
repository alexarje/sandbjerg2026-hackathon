# One-minute narrative

We are The Applonauts, and we explored the final applause at MusicLab Copenhagen. Our data came from Zenodo: motion recordings from audience members, audio from the hall, and notes on what happened on stage.

We used VS Code and Copilot to inspect the data, make plots, and build two interactive views. Applause Lab lets us move between the crowd and individual traces. In Applause Constellation, each selected motion peak becomes a note, so the room becomes a collection of rhythms we can both see and hear.

The process was quick once things worked, but we spent most of our time setting up different systems and resolving Git conflicts. Copilot helped us explore ideas, while also making it easy to lose track of what had changed.

Our takeaway: creative exploration needs shared context, careful checking, and a little patience. Thank you.
